The Studio

Bangalore's Premier Pre-Wedding Photography Studio

Firos Photography is a boutique pre-wedding photography studio based on Sarjapur Road, Bangalore, crafting cinematic couple stories for discerning Indian couples. Over the last eleven years, we've photographed 2000+ love stories at Elements Celebrate, Elements Resort, Nandi Hills, Lalbagh, Bangalore Palace, Coorg, and beyond — earning a 4.9★ rating across 456 Google reviews and a reputation as the most trusted name in luxury pre-wedding photography in South India.

What makes Firos Photography different

Most pre-wedding studios in Bangalore follow the same playbook — the same locations, the same poses, the same filter. We don't. Every shoot begins with a long conversation about who you are, how you met, and the small rituals that make your relationship yours.

Our team is trained in editorial fashion photography, documentary storytelling, and Indian cultural direction — a rare combination that produces imagery that feels both timelessly cinematic and unmistakably personal. We shoot exclusively in natural light, avoid stiff posing, and hand-grade every frame in a signature warm palette that ages beautifully.

Photography styles we offer

  • Cinematic Pre-WeddingFilm-inspired storytelling with wide anamorphic framing, motion, and mood. Ideal for couples who love visual drama.
  • Editorial Fashion Pre-WeddingMagazine-style portraiture with directed styling, structured lighting, and high-end wardrobe. For couples who see their shoot as art.
  • Candid DocumentaryUnposed, in-the-moment coverage that captures real laughter, glances, and touch. The choice for couples uneasy with the camera.
  • Traditional Indian Pre-WeddingCultural aesthetics honouring heritage — sarees, sherwanis, temple settings, marigolds, ancestral jewellery — shot with modern craft.
  • Destination Pre-WeddingMulti-day cinematic productions in Coorg, Chikmagalur, Hampi, Udaipur, Goa, and Pondicherry. Stay, travel, styling included.

The best pre-wedding locations in Bangalore

Our two most-loved signature locations are Elements Celebrate — a themed private venue with royal, floral, vintage and Bollywood-inspired sets — and Elements Resort, offering lush gardens, water features, and a blend of modern and traditional architecture perfect for cinematic love stories.

We also shoot at Nandi Hills for sunrise sessions above the clouds, Lalbagh Botanical Garden for lush morning light, Bangalore Palace for regal drama, and premium resorts across Bangalore. For adventurous couples, we travel to Coorg, Chikmagalur, Hampi, Udaipur, and Goa.

Every location is pre-scouted for its light direction, mood, and cinematic potential — and where permits are required, we handle every arrangement on your behalf.

Photographer expertise

Firos, our founder and lead photographer, brings over a decade of specialised experience in pre-wedding and wedding photography. Featured across WedMeGood, WeddingBazaar, and Google 5-star reviews, and trusted by 2000+ couples across Karnataka and India.

Our studio operates from Sarjapur Road, Bangalore with a lead photographer, second shooter, dedicated colour-grading artist, and album designers — every shoot is a team production, never outsourced. We shoot with professional cinema-grade cameras, drones, and continuous lighting for both stills and cinematic films, delivered in 2–3 weeks.
